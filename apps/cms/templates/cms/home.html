{{ party.election_name }}

Stand for {{ party.short_name }}

01

Apply online

Fill in the form, upload your photo and CV, and get a reference number.

02

Provincial review

Your provincial coordinator checks your details and referees and scores your application.

03

Executive vetting

Shortlisted applicants are interviewed by the National Executive.

04

Endorsement

Endorsed candidates receive an endorsement letter and a candidate ID card. One candidate per seat.

What you need

  1. Be a PNG citizen aged 25 or older on polling day.
  2. A clear head-and-shoulders photo and your CV.
  3. NID card or birth certificate copy, and police clearance if you have it.
  4. Two referees with phone numbers.
  5. If you belong to another party, agree to resign before nomination.
